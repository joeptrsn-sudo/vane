How Vane makes money

Yandex’s search-and-portal slice still prints the high-margin cash. E-com and taxi are bigger now, but they need fleets. We take the portal cash register and leave the warehouses.

1. Search ads

CPC on commercial queries. This is Direct. Target 70%+ gross margin. This pays for the free homepage.

2. Shopping take

Product cards in Shop intent. 6% of referred sale via Amazon/ShareASale/Impact until we have our own catalog.

3. Local pins

$49/mo SaaS for a claimed listing. No auction needed. Plumbers and dentists understand a monthly pin.

Path to first dollar

Week 1: this page on a custom domain, Plus + pin checkout via Stripe. Week 2: Bing or Brave Search API for real web results (they rent the index so you don’t build one). Week 3: affiliate product feed for Shop. Week 4: sell the first 20 Atlanta pins door-to-door. Do not crawl the web. Do not launch a browser.
